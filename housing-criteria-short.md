# SF Rental Criteria

## Location

Only north and central SF. The neighborhood has to be verifiable — if the actual location can't be confirmed, it's out.

### Accepted

**North SF**
- Presidio / Sea Cliff
- Richmond (Inner, Central, Outer)
- Marina / Cow Hollow
- Pacific Heights / Laurel Heights
- Russian Hill / Nob Hill / Telegraph Hill
- North Beach / Fisherman's Wharf
- Chinatown / Financial District

**Central SF**
- Inner Sunset / Golden Gate Heights
- Cole Valley / Haight-Ashbury
- NoPa / Western Addition
- Hayes Valley / Civic Center
- SoMa / South Beach
- Castro / Duboce Triangle / Eureka Valley
- Noe Valley
- Mission District
- Potrero Hill / Dogpatch

**South-Central**
- Twin Peaks

### Rejected

- **Tenderloin** — including anything marketed as "Lower Nob Hill" or "Union Square" that is actually south of Geary and east of Larkin. The label on the listing doesn't decide it; the address does.
- Central Sunset / Parkside, Outer Sunset / Outer Parkside
- Diamond Heights / Glen Park
- Bernal Heights
- Bayview / Hunters Point
- Ingleside / Oceanview
- Excelsior / Outer Mission
- Visitacion Valley / Portola
- Crocker-Amazon / Balboa Park

Geographic rule of thumb: nearly everything acceptable is north of ~37.745° latitude. Below ~37.735° is almost certainly a rejected neighborhood.

---

## Requirements

All four are non-negotiable:

1. **Private bathroom.** No shared baths. For a room in shared housing, the listing must explicitly say private bath or en-suite.
2. **Kitchen in the unit.** Full kitchen or kitchenette at minimum. A shared/communal kitchen does not count.
3. **Laundry in-unit or on-site.** Washer/dryer in the unit, or shared laundry in the building. A nearby laundromat does not count.
4. **Accepted neighborhood**, confirmed rather than claimed.

Ambiguity is a rejection — if a listing doesn't mention the bathroom, kitchen, or laundry, that counts as a no.

### Acceptable unit types

Studios · 1-bedrooms · rooms in shared housing (with private bath) · in-law units / ADUs / backyard cottages / basement apartments
